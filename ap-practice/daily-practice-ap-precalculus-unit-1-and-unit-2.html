Section 0: Core Concepts Review

Unit 1: Rates of Change & Concavity

1. Average Rate of Change (AROC / 平均变化率)

  • 【Formula 核心公式】 Over the interval [a,b], the AROC is defined as:

    AROC=ΔxΔy​=b−af(b)−f(a)​

  • 【三大核心意义】

    1. Algebraic (代数意义): Change in y divided by change in x.

    2. Geometric (几何意义): The Slope of the Secant Line (割线的斜率) connecting points (a,f(a)) and (b,f(b)).

    3. Real-world (现实意义): Average Speed or Average Growth Rate (过程中的平均速度).

2. Concavity (函数的凹凸性)

  • 【绝杀法则】 判断凹凸性，只看 AROC 的变化趋势 (Look at how AROC changes)!

  • Concave Up (凹向上 ∪): AROC is Increasing (变化率在不断增大).

  • Concave Down (凹向下 ∩): AROC is Decreasing (变化率在不断减小).

3. Successive Differences Method (二阶差分法)

  • 【适用条件】 Table problems where the x-interval is constant (表格题，x 是等差的).

  • 【实战推导】

    • 1st Diff (一阶差分): Δy (Consecutive y values subtracted)

    • 2nd Diff (二阶差分): Δ(Δy) (Difference of the first differences)

    • If 2nd Diff > 0 ⇒ Concave Up ∪

    • If 2nd Diff < 0 ⇒ Concave Down ∩

4. Linear vs. Quadratic (线性 vs. 二次函数)

  • Linear Functions (y=mx+b): The AROC is Constant (一阶差分为常数).

  • Quadratic Functions (y=ax2+bx+c): * The AROC itself is a Linear Function! (平均变化率本身是一次函数).

    • The rate of change of the AROC is Constant (二阶差分为常数).

Unit 2: Sequences & Exponential Functions

1. Sequences (数列的区别)

  • Arithmetic (等差): Constant difference (d). Linear behavior.

  • Geometric (等比): Constant ratio (r). Exponential behavior.

  • **If given non-consecutive terms ym​ and yn​ (where m>n):

    • Arithmetic: d=m−nym​−yn​​ (本质是算两点间的斜率)

    • Geometric: r=m−nyn​ym​​​ (直接开步数次方的根号)

**2. Exponential Functions:

  • Form: f(x)=a⋅bx. (a is initial value, b is base).

  • 【底层原理】为什么要求 b>0 且 b=1？ If b=1, 1x=1, it becomes a flat line. If b is negative (e.g., -2), (−2)1/2=−2​ is undefined in real numbers! The graph would break into non-continuous dots.

  • Domain & Range: Domain is (−∞,∞). The Range is restricted by a Horizontal Asymptote (水平渐近线).

3. Growth Rate vs Growth Factor (增长率 vs 增长因子)

  • 【易错陷阱】 Value decreases by 12% ⇒ You keep (100%−12%)=88%. The base b=0.88.

  • Exponential Growth: b=1+r (b>1) ; Exponential Decay: b=1−r (0<b<1).

**4. Continuous Compounding

  • 【底层推导】 Where does e come from? Imagine 100% interest, compounded infinitely many times a year (每时每刻都在疯狂结息): limn→∞​(1+n1​)n=e≈2.71828. It is the absolute limit of continuous growth.

  • Continuous Growth: Keyword “compounded continuously”, directly use the PERT formula: A=P⋅ert.

5. The AROC of Exponential Functions (指数函数的 AROC 规律)

  • While a linear function has a constant AROC, an exponential function’s AROC over equal-length intervals grows or decays proportionally (by the same constant multiple). (指数函数在等长区间的AROC也呈等比倍数变化)
