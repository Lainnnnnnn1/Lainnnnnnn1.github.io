我的笔记

2026年6月13日20分钟阅读

导言： 本手册针对 CIE 考卷中的 AO2（Application）材料题编写。考卷中出现的大量“未见过的物种”、“新型药物”或“罕见突变”仅为背景干扰。解题的本质是将题干中的{未知变量}代入已知的生物学逻辑链条中。阅卷标准高度机械化，遵循“一个核心词汇/逻辑链条对应一分”的原则。

考点章节：[Chapter 1: Cell Structure]

  • 题型：[新情境下的细胞分泌机制]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：一种从未见过的细胞/腺体，大量合成并分泌某种毒素/蛋白质 X}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) synthesises [Protein/Toxin X].

    2. [Protein X] is packaged into transport vesicles and moves to the Golgi body.

    3. Golgi body modifies and packages [Protein X] into secretory vesicles.

    4. Vesicles move via microtubules / cytoskeleton (using ATP).

    5. Vesicles fuse with the cell surface membrane for exocytosis.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 合成起点：提 RER (Rough endoplasmic reticulum) 和 synthesises。

    • 初级运输：提 Transport vesicles 和 moves to Golgi。

    • 加工中心：提 Golgi body 和 modifies / packages。

    • 终极运输：提 Secretory vesicles 和 microtubules / cytoskeleton。

    • 释放机制：提 Exocytosis 和 fuse with cell surface membrane。

  • 考官雷区警示：绝对不能笼统写 “vesicles move out”。必须写出物理动作：“fuse with the cell surface membrane”。严禁将 transport vesicle 与 secretory vesicle 混淆。

  • 题型：[显微镜分辨率差异]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：比较电子显微镜 (TEM/SEM) 与光学显微镜在观察某未知细胞结构时的差异}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Electron microscopes use a beam of electrons which has a shorter wavelength than light.

    2. This provides a higher resolution.

    3. It allows us to distinguish between two points that are closer together.

    4. Therefore, detailed ultrastructure (e.g., ribosomes, cristae) can be seen.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 核心原因：提 shorter wavelength。

    • 核心结果：提 higher resolution。

    • 观察极限：提 ultrastructure 或具体微小细胞器。

  • 考官雷区警示：绝不能混淆 Resolution（分辨率）和 Magnification（放大倍数）。波长短决定的是分辨率。

考点章节：[Chapter 2: Biological Molecules]

  • 题型：[极端环境下的水特性应用]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：某种耐旱植物或沙漠动物，利用水来维持生命/降温，要求解释水的特定物理性质}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Water has a high latent heat of vaporisation.

    2. Therefore, evaporation of water takes away heat energy, providing a cooling effect for [Organism Y].

    3. Water molecules form hydrogen bonds, providing cohesion.

    4. This maintains a continuous column of water in the xylem of [Plant Z].

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 蒸发散热：提 Latent heat of vaporisation 和 cooling effect。

    • 水柱拉力：提 Cohesion 和 hydrogen bonds。

  • 考官雷区警示：严禁混淆 “Latent heat of vaporisation” (蒸发散热) 与 “Specific heat capacity” (温度缓冲)。

  • 题型：[多糖结构与功能适配 (糖原/纤维素)]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：解释糖原 (储能) 或 纤维素 (结构) 的特性}

  • 万能公式 (糖原 Glycogen)：

    1. Polymer of α-glucose, highly branched due to 1,6-glycosidic bonds.

    2. Many ends for rapid addition/removal of glucose.

    3. Compact to store a large amount of energy in a small space.

    4. Insoluble, so it has no osmotic effect on the cell.

  • 万能公式 (纤维素 Cellulose)：

    1. Polymer of β-glucose, alternate glucose molecules are rotated 180°.

    2. Forms a straight chain, allowing many parallel chains to form hydrogen bonds between them.

    3. This forms microfibrils, providing high tensile strength.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 糖原：提 α-glucose, branched, compact, insoluble (no osmotic effect)。

    • 纤维素：提 β-glucose (rotated 180°), hydrogen bonds between parallel chains, microfibrils, tensile strength。

  • 考官雷区警示：纤维素必须强调“平行链之间的氢键 (hydrogen bonds between chains)”，不能写成链内部。

  • 题型：[大分子结构与功能适配 - 胶原蛋白]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：解释胶原蛋白 (Collagen) 的结构如何使其具有高抗张强度 (tensile strength)}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Every third amino acid is glycine (the smallest amino acid).

    2. This allows tight coiling to form a triple helix.

    3. The triple helix is held together by many hydrogen bonds.

    4. Many triple helices lie parallel with staggered ends.

    5. They are joined by covalent bonds / cross-links to form fibrils.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 氨基酸特性：提 Glycine 和 smallest。

    • 单分子构象：提 Triple helix 和 hydrogen bonds。

    • 多分子排列：提 Staggered ends。

    • 高阶组装：提 Covalent cross-links 和 fibrils。

  • 考官雷区警示：严禁把 Collagen 的结构称为 α-helix，必须叫 triple helix。

考点章节：[Chapter 3: Enzymes]

  • 题型：[新情境下的竞争性酶抑制剂分析]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：一种新研发的药物 X，导致某种病原体体内的关键酶 Y 活性下降}

  • 万能公式：

    1. [Drug X] has a complementary shape to the active site of [Enzyme Y].

    2. It binds to the active site, acting as a competitive inhibitor.

    3. This prevents the formation of Enzyme-Substrate Complexes (ESCs).

    4. Vmax​ remains the same, but Km​ increases indicating lower affinity.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 形状匹配：提 Complementary shape 和 active site。

    • 物理阻断：提 Competitive inhibitor 和 prevents ESCs。

    • 动力学指标：提 Vmax​ remains the same, Km​ increases, lower affinity。

  • 考官雷区警示：绝不能只写 “it fits”，必须严格使用术语 “complementary in shape”。

  • 题型：[温度或 pH 导致的酶变性]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：非最适环境导致酶促反应速率彻底归零}

  • 万能公式：

    1. High temperature / extreme pH alters or breaks ionic and hydrogen bonds.

    2. This causes a change in the tertiary structure of the enzyme.

    3. The active site shape is altered and is no longer complementary to the substrate.

    4. The enzyme is denatured, meaning no ESCs can form.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 键的破坏：提 ionic and hydrogen bonds。

    • 结构破坏：提 tertiary structure 和 no longer complementary。

    • 结果：提 denatured (不能用 killed)。

考点章节：[Chapter 4: Cell Membranes & Transport]

  • 题型：[未知信号分子的细胞传导通路]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：某种新发现的激素/配体 X 作用于靶细胞，引发了细胞内反应}

  • 万能公式：

    1. [Molecule X] binds to a specific receptor on the cell surface membrane of the target cell.

    2. The receptor has a complementary shape to [Molecule X].

    3. This activates a G-protein and triggers an enzyme cascade.

    4. This produces second messengers (e.g., cAMP), leading to signal amplification.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 膜外识别：提 Receptor on cell surface membrane 和 complementary shape。

    • 跨膜激活：提 G-protein 和 activates。

    • 级联反应：提 Enzyme cascade 和 second messengers (signal amplification)。

  • 考官雷区警示：蛋白质类激素绝不能写 “enters the cell”，必须结合在表面受体。

  • 题型：[渗透作用与植物细胞吸水/失水]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：土豆块/植物组织放入未知溶液中质量改变，或红细胞胀破}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Water moves across the partially permeable membrane by osmosis.

    2. Water moves down a water potential gradient (from a higher/less negative to a lower/more negative water potential).

    3. [If hypertonic]: Cell loses water and becomes plasmolysed (protoplast shrinks).

    4. [If hypotonic]: Cell gains water and becomes turgid (or bursts if animal cell).

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 运输方式：提 Osmosis 和 partially permeable membrane。

    • 动力方向：提 down a water potential gradient。

  • 考官雷区警示：只要提水，绝对禁止写 “concentration gradient”，必须是 water potential gradient。

考点章节：[Chapter 5: The Mitotic Cell Cycle]

  • 题型：[抗肿瘤药物的细胞周期阻断]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：化疗药物 X 被用于治疗某种罕见癌症，它作用于纺锤丝或 DNA 复制阶段}

  • 万能公式：

    1. [Drug X] prevents DNA replication OR prevents spindle fibre formation.

    2. The cell cycle is arrested at [Interphase / Metaphase].

    3. Sister chromatids cannot separate OR centromeres cannot divide.

    4. This prevents mitosis / cell division, stopping the formation of a tumour / clonal expansion.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 靶向阶段：提 DNA replication / Spindle fibre。

    • 周期阻断：提 Interphase / Metaphase。

    • 染色体异常：提 Sister chromatids 和 cannot separate / divide。

    • 宏观结果：提 prevents clonal expansion / tumour。

  • 考官雷区警示：描述有丝分裂后期阻断时，必须精确指明是 “sister chromatids” 无法分离。

  • 题型：[端粒与干细胞功能]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：解释某细胞群能无限分裂的原因，或衰老的机制}

  • 万能公式 (端粒 Telomeres)：

    1. Telomeres are placed at the ends of chromosomes to prevent the loss of genes.

    2. They allow cells to divide continuously without losing important genetic information during DNA replication.

  • 万能公式 (干细胞 Stem Cells)：

    1. They are undifferentiated / unspecialised cells.

    2. They can divide by mitosis to provide a continuous supply of cells.

    3. They can differentiate / specialise into specific cell types for tissue repair.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 端粒：提 prevent loss of genes。

    • 干细胞：提 undifferentiated 和 differentiate into specific cells。

考点章节：[Chapter 6: Nucleic Acids & Protein Synthesis]

  • 题型：[基因突变导致的蛋白质失活机制]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：基因序列中发生了一个核苷酸替换，导致产生的酶/受体 X 完全失效}

  • 万能公式：

    1. A change in the nucleotide sequence leads to a different codon on the mRNA.

    2. This codes for a different amino acid, altering the primary structure of [Protein X].

    3. Different R-groups interact differently, altering ionic / hydrogen / disulfide bonds.

    4. The tertiary structure changes, meaning the active site/receptor is no longer complementary.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 密码子与序列：提 Codon 和 amino acid / primary structure。

    • 键的破坏：提 R-groups 和 ionic / hydrogen / disulfide bonds。

    • 构象失效：提 Tertiary structure 和 no longer complementary。

  • 考官雷区警示：不可跳跃逻辑。必须按照 “DNA -> codon -> amino acid -> bonds -> tertiary structure” 连贯书写。

  • 题型：[RNA 剪接 (为何 mRNA 比基因短)]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：对比某真核生物细胞核内 DNA 长度与游离在细胞质中的 mRNA 长度}

  • 万能公式：

    1. The gene contains introns which are non-coding sequences.

    2. These introns are removed from the primary transcript.

    3. This occurs during RNA splicing.

    4. Only exons (coding sequences) are joined together to form the mature mRNA.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 无用序列：提 introns (non-coding)。

    • 处理过程：提 removed from primary transcript 和 RNA splicing。

    • 最终产物：提 exons joined together。

  • 考官雷区警示：严禁写 “DNA splicing”，必须是 RNA splicing。

考点章节：[Chapter 7: Transport in Plants]

  • 题型：[韧皮部装载 (Phloem Loading)]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：光合产物 (蔗糖) 如何从叶肉细胞移动到筛管中}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Protons (H+) are pumped out of the companion cell into the cell wall using ATP via active transport.

    2. This creates a proton gradient.

    3. Protons diffuse back into the companion cell through a cotransporter protein, bringing sucrose with them.

    4. Sucrose moves into the sieve tube element via plasmodesmata.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 主动建立梯度：提 Protons pumped out 和 active transport (ATP)。

    • 协同运输：提 cotransporter protein。

    • 进入筛管：提 plasmodesmata。

  • 考官雷区警示：Cotransporter 是极其重要的得分词，进入筛管是通过 Plasmodesmata (胞间连丝) 的扩散，而非主动运输。

  • 题型：[环境变量对蒸腾拉力的影响]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：某种植物在极端高温/强风下，水分流失速率发生变化}

  • 万能公式：

    1. [High temperature] provides more kinetic energy.

    2. Increases the rate of evaporation of water from mesophyll cell walls.

    3. This creates a steeper water vapour potential gradient.

    4. Rate of diffusion of water vapour out through stomata increases.

    5. This increases the tension / negative pressure in the xylem, drawing water up via mass flow.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 状态改变 (蒸发)：提 Evaporation from mesophyll cell walls。

    • 气体离开 (扩散)：提 diffusion through stomata。

    • 拉力补充：提 tension / mass flow。

  • 考官雷区警示：必须区分：从细胞壁是 “evaporation (蒸发)“，从气孔是 “diffusion (扩散)“。

考点章节：[Chapter 8: Transport in Mammals]

  • 题型：[代谢变化引发的波尔效应]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：动物在剧烈运动/缺氧环境下，血红蛋白释放氧气的能力增强}

  • 万能公式：

    1. More CO2​ diffuses into the red blood cell from respiring tissues.

    2. Carbonic anhydrase catalyses the reaction of CO2​ and H2​O to form carbonic acid.

    3. Carbonic acid dissociates into H+ and HCO3−​.

    4. Haemoglobin binds to H+ to form haemoglobinic acid (HHb).

    5. This causes an allosteric effect, decreasing haemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen, causing O2​ release.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 酶促反应：提 More CO2​ 和 Carbonic anhydrase。

    • 离子解离：提 dissociates into H+。

    • 构象改变：提 binds to H+ (HHb) 和 allosteric effect。

    • 释放氧气：提 decreased affinity。

  • 考官雷区警示：首句不写 “More” 直接扣分。严禁说 CO2​ 挤占氧气位置，必须用 decreased affinity。

  • 题型：[心动周期与瓣膜的物理开闭]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：看图解释心房/心室收缩时，房室瓣(AV)和半月瓣的开闭逻辑}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Ventricles contract, causing volume to decrease and pressure to rise sharply.

    2. Pressure in the ventricle becomes higher than the pressure in the atrium.

    3. This pressure difference forces the atrioventricular (AV) valve to close (preventing backflow).

    4. Pressure in the ventricle becomes higher than the pressure in the aorta/pulmonary artery.

    5. This forces the semilunar valve to open.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 压力差对比：必须写 higher than (如 Ventricle pressure > Atrium pressure)。

    • 防止倒流：提 prevents backflow。

  • 考官雷区警示：严禁写“心室把血挤过去导致瓣膜开关”，瓣膜开闭的唯一得分词是“两腔室的压力对比 (Pressure differences)”。

考点章节：[Chapter 9: Gas Exchange]

  • 题型：[气道组织的分布与功能适配]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：吸烟或某种疾病导致气道壁组织受损，要求解释原本组织的生理功能}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Cartilage: keeps the airway open / prevents collapse during inspiration.

    2. Smooth muscle: contracts to constrict the airway (controls air flow).

    3. Elastic fibres: stretch during inspiration and recoil during expiration to force air out (prevent bursting).

    4. Goblet cells / Mucous glands: secrete mucus to trap pathogens and dust.

    5. Ciliated cells: cilia waft / move mucus upwards away from the alveoli.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 软骨支撑：提 Cartilage 和 prevents collapse。

    • 平滑肌/弹性纤维：提 Smooth muscle contracts，提 Elastic fibres stretch and recoil。

    • 清扫系统：提 Goblet cells secrete mucus，提 cilia waft upwards。

  • 考官雷区警示：Elastic fibres 的动词只能用 stretch and recoil，绝不能用 contract/relax（肌肉专属）。Cilia 必须写明向上(upwards)摆动方向。

  • 题型：[肺泡的结构适配 (Alveoli adaptations)]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：解释肺部如何实现高效的气体交换}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Many alveoli provide a large surface area for diffusion.

    2. Alveolar wall is consisting of squamous epithelium which is one cell thick.

    3. Capillary wall is also one cell thick, providing a short diffusion pathway.

    4. Well supplied with a network of blood capillaries to maintain a steep concentration gradient.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 面积与厚度：提 large surface area, squamous epithelium, one cell thick。

    • 路径与梯度：提 short diffusion pathway, steep concentration gradient。

  • 考官雷区警示：不能只写 “thin wall”，必须写 “one cell thick” 或 “short diffusion pathway”。

考点章节：[Chapter 10: Infectious Diseases]

  • 题型：[超级细菌耐药性的演化机制]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：一种新型抗生素 X 被广泛使用后，医院内出现了能够存活的耐药菌株 Y}

  • 万能公式：

    1. A random mutation occurs in the bacterial DNA, producing a resistance allele.

    2. The presence of [Antibiotic X] acts as a selection pressure.

    3. Resistant bacteria survive and reproduce, while susceptible bacteria are killed.

    4. Resistant bacteria pass the resistance allele to their offspring.

    5. The allele frequency increases in the population over generations.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 基因溯源：提 Random mutation 和 resistance allele。

    • 环境筛选：提 Selection pressure。

    • 遗传积累：提 Survive and reproduce 和 allele frequency increases。

  • 考官雷区警示：抗生素不会“导致”突变，只能充当选择压力。不能写 “pass the gene”，必须用严谨的 “pass the allele (等位基因)”。

  • 题型：[青霉素的杀菌机制及病毒免疫原因]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：解释抗生素(如青霉素)如何工作，或者为何对病毒无效}

  • 万能公式 (杀菌机制)：

    1. Penicillin inhibits the enzyme transpeptidase.

    2. This prevents the formation of cross-links between peptidoglycan chains.

    3. The cell wall becomes weak, water enters by osmosis, and the cell bursts (osmotic lysis).

  • 万能公式 (病毒无效)：

    1. Viruses do not have a cell wall (no peptidoglycan).

    2. Viruses do not have their own metabolism or ribosomes (antibiotics often target these).

    3. Viruses replicate inside host cells where antibiotics cannot reach them.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 细菌死因：提 inhibits transpeptidase, prevents cross-links, osmotic lysis。

    • 病毒免疫：提 no cell wall / acellular, no metabolism / ribosomes。

考点章节：[Chapter 11: Immunity]

  • 题型：[巨噬细胞清除未知病原体机制]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：某种未知的细菌/病毒侵入人体后，吞噬细胞如何将其降解}

  • 万能公式：

    1. [Pathogen Z] binds to receptors on the macrophage.

    2. Macrophage engulfs [Pathogen Z] by endocytosis / phagocytosis.

    3. A phagosome (phagocytic vacuole) is formed inside the macrophage.

    4. Lysosomes fuse with the phagosome.

    5. Lysosomes release hydrolytic enzymes to digest/break down [Pathogen Z].

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 识别与囊泡：提 Endocytosis / phagocytosis 和 phagosome。

    • 溶酶体破坏：提 Lysosomes fuse with phagosome 和 hydrolytic enzymes。

  • 考官雷区警示：必须指明 “lysosomes fuse with phagosome”，不能孤立地写“释放酶”。必须用 “hydrolytic enzymes”，不可用笼统的 digestive enzymes。

  • 题型：[单克隆抗体 (mAbs) 的制备 (Hybridoma)]

  • 触发情境：{题干描述：医药公司需要大规模生产针对某种毒素/癌症的特异性抗体}

  • 万能公式：

    1. Inject the specific antigen into a small mammal (e.g., a mouse).

    2. Extract plasma cells (B-lymphocytes) from the spleen of the mouse.

    3. Fuse the plasma cells with myeloma cells (cancer cells).

    4. This forms hybridoma cells which can divide continuously and secrete antibodies.

    5. Screen for the desired hybridoma and clone it for large-scale production.

  • 得分点锁定锚点：

    • 免疫激活：提 inject antigen。

    • 细胞融合：提 plasma cells fused with myeloma cells。

    • 终极产物：提 hybridoma cells。

  • 考官雷区警示：注射的必须是 “antigen”（抗原），提取的必须是 “plasma cells”（浆细胞，不能笼统写 white blood cells）。
