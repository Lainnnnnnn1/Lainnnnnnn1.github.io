Chapter 1: Cell Structure (细胞结构)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Resolution分辨率The ability to distinguish between two points that are very close together. (电子显微镜分辨率高是因为波长短)
Magnification放大倍数The number of times larger an image is compared to the real size of the object. 公式: M=I/A.
Organelle细胞器A functionally and structurally distinct part of a cell.
Nucleolus核仁Structure inside the nucleus responsible for the synthesis of ribosomes (rRNA).
Lysosome溶酶体Vesicle containing hydrolytic enzymes to break down old organelles or pathogens.
Golgi Body高尔基体Modifies, sorts, and packages proteins for secretion. (常考糖基化 glycosylation)
Plasmodesmata胞间连丝Gaps in cell walls where cytoplasm of adjacent plant cells connects. (韧皮部装载的最后一步通道)
Tonoplast液泡膜The partially permeable membrane surrounding the central vacuole in plant cells.

Chapter 2: Biological Molecules (生物分子)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Monomer / Polymer单体 / 聚合物Monomer is a simple molecule used as a basic building block for the synthesis of a polymer.
Macromolecule生物大分子A large biological molecule such as a protein, polysaccharide, or nucleic acid.
Glycosidic Bond糖苷键A C-O-C bond formed between two monosaccharides by condensation. (水解时断裂)
Peptide / Phosphodiester肽键 / 磷酸二酯键Peptide bond links amino acids; Phosphodiester bond links nucleotides.
Triglyceride甘油三酯Lipid formed by one glycerol and three fatty acids via ester bonds. (主要用于储能)
Tertiary Structure三级结构The 3D folding of a polypeptide chain held by interactions between R-groups. (决定酶活性位点的形状)
Hydrophobic Interaction疏水相互作用Association between non-polar R-groups to exclude water. (常发生于球状蛋白中心)
Latent Heat of Vaporisation汽化潜热The energy required to turn liquid water into gas. (水具有很高的汽化潜热，用于出汗/蒸腾降温)

Chapter 3: Enzymes (酶)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Activation Energy活化能The minimum energy required to start a chemical reaction. (酶通过降低活化能起作用)
Active Site活性位点The specific region of an enzyme where the substrate binds.
Complementary(形状) 互补Shapes that fit together specifically (e.g., active site and substrate). (严禁写 “fits”，必须写 complementary)
Allosteric Site变构位点A site on an enzyme other than the active site where an inhibitor binds. (非竞争性抑制剂结合处)
Vmax​最大反应速率The maximum rate of reaction when all enzyme active sites are saturated.
Km​ (Michaelis constant)米氏常数The substrate concentration at which the reaction rate is half of Vmax​. (衡量对底物亲和力 affinity 的指标)
Denaturation变性The loss of 3D structure due to breaking of specific bonds. (严禁说酶被 killed)

Chapter 4: Cell Membranes & Transport (细胞膜与运输)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Fluid Mosaic Model流动镶嵌模型Phospholipids are fluid; proteins are the mosaic.
Water Potential水势The tendency of water to move from one area to another. (渗透作用必须顺着水势梯度进行)
Active Transport主动运输Movement of substances against a concentration gradient using ATP and carrier proteins.
Facilitated Diffusion协助扩散Passive movement down a concentration gradient using transport proteins (carrier or channel).
Aquaporin水通道蛋白Channel proteins that specifically facilitate the rapid passage of water.
Endocytosis / Exocytosis胞吞 / 胞吐Bulk transport of materials into/out of a cell via vesicle formation/fusion (requires ATP).
Plasmolysis质壁分离The pulling away of the cytoplasm and cell membrane from the cell wall due to water loss.

Chapter 5: The Mitotic Cell Cycle (细胞周期)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Interphase间期Phase of cell cycle including G1, S (DNA replication), and G2 phases.
Mitosis有丝分裂Nuclear division producing two genetically identical daughter nuclei.
Cytokinesis胞质分裂The division of the cytoplasm to form two separate daughter cells.
Centromere着丝点Region of a chromosome where sister chromatids are joined. (后期 Anaphase 必须分裂)
Chromatid染色单体One of the two identical copies of a chromosome post-replication.
Kinetochore动粒 / 着丝点结构Protein structure on centromeres where spindle fibres attach.
Telomere端粒Repetitive DNA sequence at ends of chromosomes protecting genes from being lost.
Stem Cell干细胞Undifferentiated cells capable of continuous division and specialisation.

Chapter 6: Nucleic Acids (核酸与蛋白质合成)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Nucleotide核苷酸Monomer of nucleic acids, consisting of a pentose sugar, a phosphate group, and a nitrogenous base.
Purine / Pyrimidine嘌呤 / 嘧啶Purines (A, G) have a double ring; Pyrimidines (C, T, U) have a single ring.
Semi-conservative半保留复制Replication where each new molecule has one original and one new strand.
DNA PolymeraseDNA聚合酶Enzyme that catalyses the formation of phosphodiester bonds between nucleotides.
Splicing (RNA splicing)RNA 剪接Removal of introns and joining of exons to form mature mRNA. (严禁写 DNA splicing)
Intron / Exon内含子 / 外显子Introns are non-coding sequences; Exons are coding sequences.
Transcription / Translation转录 / 翻译Transcription makes mRNA from DNA; Translation makes polypeptide from mRNA.
Codon / Anticodon密码子 / 反密码子A sequence of 3 bases on mRNA (codon) or tRNA (anticodon) coding for an amino acid.

Chapter 7: Transport in Plants (植物运输)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Xylem / Phloem木质部 / 韧皮部Xylem transports water and minerals; Phloem transports assimilates (sucrose/amino acids).
Companion Cell伴胞Cell closely associated with a sieve tube element, providing ATP for phloem loading.
Cohesion内聚力Attraction between water molecules by hydrogen bonding. (维持水柱不断裂)
Adhesion附着力Attraction between water molecules and the xylem wall. (防止水柱下沉)
Transpiration蒸腾作用Loss of water vapour from plant leaves by evaporation of water.
Apoplast / Symplast质外体 / 共质体Apoplast: through cell walls (stops at Casparian strip). Symplast: through cytoplasm and plasmodesmata.
Sink / Source库 / 源Source produces assimilates (e.g., leaf); Sink uses/stores assimilates (e.g., root).
Xerophyte旱生植物A plant adapted to survive in conditions with very little liquid water.

Chapter 8: Transport in Mammals (哺乳动物运输)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Tissue Fluid组织液Fluid surrounding the cells of multicellular animals, formed from blood plasma.
Lymph淋巴液Fluid found inside lymphatic vessels, formed from tissue fluid.
Atrioventricular Valve房室瓣Valve between atrium and ventricle, preventing backflow of blood into atrium.
Semilunar Valve半月瓣Valve at the base of aorta and pulmonary artery, preventing backflow into ventricles.
Allosteric Effect变构效应Binding at one site changes the shape of the entire molecule. (如 H+ 结合导致血红蛋白释放 O2​)
Bohr Effect波尔效应Decrease in Hb affinity for oxygen due to high CO2​ (or low pH).
Carbonic Anhydrase碳酸酐酶Enzyme in RBC catalysing reaction of CO2​ and H2​O to form carbonic acid.
Systole / Diastole收缩期 / 舒张期Contraction (systole) and relaxation (diastole) of heart chambers.
Sinoatrial Node (SAN)窦房结The pacemaker of the heart in the wall of the right atrium.

Chapter 9: Gas Exchange (气体交换)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Trachea / Bronchus气管 / 支气管Main airways leading to the lungs, supported by C-shaped/irregular rings of cartilage.
Alveoli肺泡Tiny air sacs in the lungs where gas exchange occurs.
Cartilage软骨Tissue in trachea/bronchi preventing airway collapse.
Goblet Cell杯状细胞Cell in epithelium that synthesises and secretes mucus.
Squamous Epithelium鳞状上皮Thin, flattened cells forming the alveoli wall (one cell thick).
Elastic Fibres弹性纤维Fibres that stretch during inspiration and recoil during expiration to force air out.

Chapter 10: Infectious Diseases (传染病)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Pathogen病原体A disease-causing organism (e.g., bacteria, virus, fungi).
Cholera / Malaria霍乱 / 疟疾Cholera is caused by Vibrio cholerae (bacterium); Malaria is caused by Plasmodium (protoctist).
Tuberculosis (TB)结核病Airborne infectious disease caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis.
Vector媒介An organism that transmits a pathogen (e.g., Anopheles mosquito for malaria).
Antibiotic Resistance抗生素耐药性Ability of bacteria to survive exposure to antibiotics due to random mutation.
Endemic地方性的A disease constantly present in a certain population or region.

Chapter 11: Immunity (免疫)

核心词汇 (Term)中文释义英文定义与考点语境 (Context)
Macrophage巨噬细胞Large phagocytic cell that engulfs pathogens and acts as an antigen-presenting cell.
B-lymphocyteB淋巴细胞White blood cell that matures in bone marrow and produces antibodies (via plasma cells).
T-lymphocyteT淋巴细胞White blood cell that matures in thymus; includes T-helper and T-killer cells.
Memory Cell记忆细胞Lymphocyte that remains in the blood to provide long-term immunological memory.
Phagocytosis吞噬作用Process of engulfing pathogens by macrophages forming a phagosome.
Antigenic Variation抗原变异Changes in pathogens’ surface proteins to evade immune response. (导致疫苗失效或反复感染)
Hybridoma杂交瘤细胞A cell formed by fusing a plasma cell and a myeloma cell. (用于生产单抗)
Active / Passive Immunity主动 / 被动免疫Active: body makes its own antibodies; Passive: body receives ready-made antibodies.
