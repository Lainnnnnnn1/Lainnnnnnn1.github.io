1. Thermodynamics

1.1 核心定义 (Definitions) - ⭐ 必须一字不差

1.1 Core Definitions - ⭐ Must be memorised exactly

标准条件 (Standard Conditions): 298 K, 100 kPa.
Standard Conditions: 298 K, 100 kPa.

第一电离能 (First Ionization Energy):
Energy required to remove one electron from one mole of gaseous atoms to form one mole of gaseous 1+ ions.

生成焓 (Enthalpy of Formation):
Enthalpy change when 1 mole of a substance is formed from its elements in their standard states under standard conditions.

晶格生成焓 (Lattice Formation Enthalpy):
Enthalpy change when 1 mole of a solid ionic compound is formed from its gaseous ions. (Always exothermic, ΔH < 0)

晶格解离焓 (Lattice Dissociation Enthalpy):
Enthalpy change to separate 1 mole of solid ionic compound into its gaseous ions. (Always endothermic, ΔH > 0)

原子化焓 (Enthalpy of Atomisation):
Enthalpy change to form 1 mole of gaseous atoms from the element in its standard state.

电子亲和能 (Electron Affinity)
1st EA: O(g) + e⁻ → O⁻(g) (Exothermic)
2nd EA: O⁻(g) + e⁻ → O²⁻(g) (Endothermic)

⭐ Why 2nd EA is endothermic?
Energy is required to overcome repulsion between negative electron and negative ion.

1.2 晶格能与键型 (Bonding)

1.2 Lattice Energy & Bonding

完美离子模型 (Perfect Ionic Model): Assumes ions are point charges and perfectly spherical.
Perfect Ionic Model: ions treated as spherical point charges.

共价成分 (Covalent Character)
Phenomenon: Experimental lattice energy is more exothermic than theoretical value.

⭐ Explanation Template:

  • The cation (e.g., Ag⁺) is small/high charge density.
  • The anion (e.g., I⁻) is large.
  • Cation polarises the anion (distorts electron cloud).
  • This gives covalent character → stronger bonding than predicted.

1.3 熵与自由能 (Entropy & Gibbs Free Energy)

1.3 Entropy & Gibbs Free Energy

熵 ΔS: Measure of disorder (J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹).
ΔG = ΔH - TΔS (note ΔS must ÷1000 to convert to kJ).
Feasible when ΔG ≤ 0.

⭐ Why ΔG vs T graph has slope -ΔS?
From y = mx + c → ΔG = (-ΔS)T + ΔH.

2.1 Measuring Standard Electrode Potentials

条件: 298 K, 100 kPa, 1 mol dm⁻³

2.2 电池符号与计算

2.2 Cell Notation & Calculations

Convention: ROOR (Reduced | Oxidised || Oxidised | Reduced)
Ecell = E(right) – E(left)

Most positive E° → strongest oxidising agent (reduction)
Most negative E° → strongest reducing agent (oxidation)

2.3 燃料电池 (Fuel Cells) ⭐ 必背

2.3 Fuel Cells ⭐ Must Memorise

酸性乙醇燃料电池 (Acidic Ethanol)

(-) C₂H₅OH + 3H₂O → 2CO₂ + 12H⁺ + 12e⁻
(+) O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H₂O
Total: C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O

碱性氢氧燃料电池 (Alkaline Hydrogen)

(-) 2H₂ + 4OH⁻ → 4H₂O + 4e⁻
(+) O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻

Advantages: high efficiency, continuous supply
Disadvantages: H₂ storage difficulty

2.4 锂离子电池 (Lithium-ion)

2.4 Lithium-ion Cell

放电 (Discharge):
(-) Li → Li⁺ + e⁻
(+) Li⁺ + CoO₂ + e⁻ → LiCoO₂

充电 (Recharge): reverse
(-) Li⁺ + e⁻ → Li
(+) LiCoO₂ → Li⁺ + CoO₂ + e⁻

3. 酸碱平衡 (Acids & Bases)

3. Acids & Bases

pH = –log[H⁺]
Kw = 1.0×10⁻¹⁴
Weak acid: Ka ≈ [H⁺]² / [HA]

Buffer:
[H⁺] = Ka × [Acid]/[Salt]

⭐ Principles:
Add H⁺ → reacts with A⁻ → forms HA
Add OH⁻ → reacts with HA → forms A⁻
