我刚刚重新调取了 2022-2023 年所有 Paper 2 的 MS 和 ER（考官报告），为你逐章地毯式扫荡，整理出这套**【11章全覆盖：CIE AS 生物终极满分默写清单】**。

只要题干出现触发词，不要思考，直接把下面的黑体字默写上去，一字不差，拿全分。

Chapter 1: Cell Structure (细胞结构)

题型 1：蛋白质分泌路径 (Protein Secretion Pathway)

  • 触发词：Describe the role of organelles in the production and release of proteins.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) synthesises polypeptide / protein.
    2. Protein is packaged into transport vesicles and moves to the Golgi body.
    3. Golgi body modifies (e.g., glycosylation) and packages protein into secretory vesicles.
    4. Vesicles move via microtubules / cytoskeleton (requires ATP).
    5. Vesicles fuse with the cell surface membrane for exocytosis.
  • 💀 考官避坑：绝不能写“vesicles exocytosis”，必须写“vesicles fuse with cell surface membrane”。

题型 2：显微镜对比 (Microscopy)

  • 触发词：Advantage of TEM over light microscope / SEM.
  • 满分默写：
    1. TEM has higher resolution and higher magnification.
    2. Because electron beam has a shorter wavelength than light.
    3. Can see smaller organelles (e.g., ribosomes / internal structure of mitochondria).

Chapter 2: Biological Molecules (生物分子)

题型 3：糖类结构与功能 (Glycogen / Cellulose)

  • 触发词：Relate structure of glycogen/cellulose to its function.
  • 满分默写 (Glycogen 储能)：
    1. Polymer of α-glucose, joined by 1,4 and 1,6 glycosidic bonds.
    2. Highly branched → many ends for rapid addition/removal of glucose.
    3. Compact → stores large amount of energy in a small space.
    4. Insoluble → has no osmotic effect on the cell.
  • 满分默写 (Cellulose 结构)：
    1. Polymer of β-glucose, joined by 1,4 glycosidic bonds.
    2. Alternate glucose molecules are rotated 180° → forms a straight chain.
    3. Many hydrogen bonds between parallel chains form microfibrils → provides high tensile strength.

题型 4：水的特性 (Properties of Water)

  • 触发词：Importance of hydrogen bonding in water.
  • 满分默写：
    1. High latent heat of vaporisation → evaporation provides a cooling effect.
    2. Cohesion between water molecules → maintains a continuous column in xylem.

Chapter 3: Enzymes (酶)

题型 5：抑制剂与 Km​/Vmax​ (Inhibitors)

  • 触发词：Explain the effect of competitive / non-competitive inhibitor.
  • 满分默写 (Competitive)：
    1. Inhibitor has a similar shape to the substrate.
    2. Binds to the active site → fewer Enzyme-Substrate Complexes (ESCs) form.
    3. Vmax​ stays the same, but Km​ increases (lower affinity).
  • 满分默写 (Non-competitive)：
    1. Binds to the allosteric site → alters the tertiary structure of the enzyme.
    2. Active site changes shape and is no longer complementary to the substrate.
    3. Vmax​ decreases, Km​ stays the same.

Chapter 4: Cell Membranes & Transport (细胞膜与运输)

题型 6：细胞信号传导 (Cell Signalling)

  • 触发词：Explain how a signalling molecule causes a response.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Signalling molecule (ligand) binds to a specific receptor on the cell surface membrane.
    2. Receptor has a complementary shape to the ligand.
    3. Binding activates a G-protein → triggers an enzyme cascade (e.g., cAMP production).
    4. This leads to signal amplification.

题型 7：渗透作用 (Osmosis)

  • 触发词：Explain the change in mass of potato in sucrose solution.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Water moves by osmosis down a water potential gradient (from higher to lower water potential).
    2. Through a partially permeable membrane (phospholipid bilayer / aquaporins).
  • 💀 考官避坑：只要提到水，绝对禁止写 “concentration gradient”，必须写 “water potential gradient”！

Chapter 5: The Mitotic Cell Cycle (细胞周期)

题型 8：端粒与干细胞 (Telomeres & Stem Cells)

  • 触发词：Role of telomeres / stem cells.
  • 满分默写 (Telomeres)：
    1. Prevent the loss of genes / genetic information during DNA replication.
    2. Allow cells to divide continuously without losing coding DNA.
  • 满分默写 (Stem Cells)：
    1. They are undifferentiated / unspecialised cells.
    2. Divide by mitosis to provide a continuous supply of cells.
    3. Can differentiate / specialise into specific cell types (e.g., for tissue repair).

Chapter 6: Nucleic Acids & Protein Synthesis (核酸与蛋白质)

题型 9：DNA 复制酶的作用 (DNA Polymerase & Ligase)

  • 触发词：Role of enzymes in DNA replication.
  • 满分默写：
    1. DNA Polymerase: aligns complementary nucleotides and forms phosphodiester bonds between adjacent nucleotides.
    2. DNA Ligase: joins Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand by forming phosphodiester bonds.

题型 10：转录后修饰 (Post-transcriptional Modification)

  • 触发词：Explain why mRNA is shorter than the gene.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Gene contains introns (non-coding sequences) and exons (coding sequences).
    2. Introns are removed from the primary transcript during RNA splicing.
    3. Only exons are joined together to form mature mRNA.
  • 💀 考官避坑：严禁写 “DNA splicing”，必须写 “RNA splicing” 或 “splicing of primary transcript”。

Chapter 7: Transport in Plants (植物运输)

题型 11：韧皮部装载 (Phloem Loading - 必考大题)

  • 触发词：Explain how sucrose is loaded into phloem sieve tubes.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Protons (H+) are pumped out of the companion cell into the cell wall using ATP (active transport).
    2. This creates a proton gradient.
    3. Protons diffuse back into the companion cell through a cotransporter protein, bringing sucrose with them (against sucrose’s concentration gradient).
    4. Sucrose diffuses into the sieve tube element via plasmodesmata.

Chapter 8: Transport in Mammals (哺乳动物运输)

题型 12：波尔效应 (The Bohr Shift - 必考大题)

  • 触发词：Explain how CO2​ causes the release of O2​ from haemoglobin.
  • 满分默写：
    1. More CO2​ diffuses into the red blood cell.
    2. Carbonic anhydrase catalyses the reaction of CO2​ and H2​O to form carbonic acid (H2​CO3​).
    3. Carbonic acid dissociates into H+ and HCO3−​.
    4. Haemoglobin binds to H+ to form haemoglobinic acid (HHb).
    5. This causes an allosteric effect (changes tertiary structure of Hb), decreasing its affinity for oxygen → O2​ is released.
  • 💀 考官避坑：第一句话必须写 “More CO2​“，不写 More 直接扣分！

Chapter 9: Gas Exchange (气体交换)

题型 13：气道组织的分布与功能 (Airway Tissues)

  • 触发词：Role of cartilage / smooth muscle / elastic fibres / mucus.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Cartilage: keeps the airway open / prevents collapse during inspiration.
    2. Smooth muscle: contracts to constrict the airway (control air flow).
    3. Elastic fibres: stretch during inspiration and recoil during expiration to force air out (prevent bursting).
    4. Goblet cells / Mucous glands: secrete mucus to trap pathogens/dust.
    5. Ciliated cells: cilia waft/move mucus upwards away from the alveoli.
  • 💀 考官避坑：Elastic fibres 只能用 stretch and recoil，绝不能用 contract/relax（那是肌肉的词）！

Chapter 10: Infectious Diseases (传染病)

题型 14：抗药性的产生 (Antibiotic Resistance)

  • 触发词：Explain how bacteria become resistant to antibiotics.
  • 满分默写：
    1. A random mutation occurs, producing a resistance allele.
    2. The antibiotic acts as a selection pressure.
    3. Resistant bacteria survive and reproduce (susceptible ones die).
    4. They pass the resistance allele to their offspring.
    5. The allele frequency increases in the population.

Chapter 11: Immunity (免疫)

题型 15：吞噬作用 (Phagocytosis)

  • 触发词：Describe how macrophages engulf and destroy bacteria.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Pathogen binds to receptors on the macrophage.
    2. Macrophage engulfs pathogen by endocytosis / phagocytosis, forming a phagosome (phagocytic vacuole).
    3. Lysosomes fuse with the phagosome.
    4. Release hydrolytic enzymes into the phagosome to digest/break down the pathogen.

题型 16：单克隆抗体制备 (Hybridoma Method)

  • 触发词：Outline the production of monoclonal antibodies.
  • 满分默写：
    1. Inject antigen into a small mammal (e.g., mouse) to stimulate an immune response.
    2. Extract plasma cells (B-lymphocytes) from the spleen.
    3. Fuse plasma cells with myeloma cells (cancer cells) to form hybridoma cells.
    4. Screen/select the hybridoma cells that produce the desired antibody.
    5. Clone the selected hybridoma cells for large-scale production.

💡 终极嘱咐（给 2026 考生的保命法则）：

  1. 遇到图表题 (Data Response)：只要题目有图表，必须引用数据（Data Quote）！引用数据必须带单位！(例如：At 20°C, the rate is 45 arbitrary units).
  2. 遇到 Compare 题：必须写出 Similarities（相同点） 和 Differences（不同点），缺一不可。
  3. 遇到 Evaluate 题：必须写出 Supports the conclusion（支持的证据） 和 Does NOT support the conclusion（不支持的证据，如：没有统计学检验、样本量太小、没有误差棒）。
